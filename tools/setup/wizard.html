Step 1 of 5 · Get ready

This setup installs ACL Lens on your ServiceNow instance. It takes about 5–10 minutes. Nothing is changed on your instance until step 4.

What you need

  • A ServiceNow instance where you can sign in as an administrator. A free Personal Developer Instance works well. Get a free instance
  • Your instance address, for example https://dev12345.service-now.com, plus your user name and password.

This computer

  • Checking Node.js…
  • Checking the ServiceNow SDK…
What gets installed here?

The ServiceNow SDK and the build tools listed in package.json, at the exact versions in package-lock.json. They are saved in this folder only (node_modules). Nothing is installed system-wide.

Step 3 of 5 · Choose the app scope

                    

                        Every custom ServiceNow app has a scope: a unique name that prefixes its tables, roles and settings. It normally starts with your
                        instance's company code, so we look that up for you.
                    

                    
Looking at your instance… (this can take up to 20 seconds)


                    

                        What should the setup do?
                        

                    


                    

                        
                        
                        
Lowercase letters, numbers and underscores, up to 18 characters. Keep the suggestion unless you have a reason to change it.

                        

                    


                    

                        
                        
                        

                            Saved in acl-lens.local.json on this computer only (it is never committed). Later updates run with npm run deploy.
                        

                    


                    

                        Why not use the scope in the source code?
                        

                            The source uses the placeholder x_acllens so anyone can share it publicly. The setup builds a private copy with your scope in
                            .deploy/, so your company code never ends up in the shared code.
